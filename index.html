Metabolite • Phase 1

Ribulose-1,5-bisphosphate (RuBP)

5C
Formula / EC Number
C₅H₁₂O₁₁P₂
Energetics (ΔG'°)
-35.1 kJ/mol
Biochemical Reaction
RuBP (5C) + CO₂ + H₂O → 2 × 3-PGA (3C)
Mechanism & Physiological Role

The primary 5-carbon CO₂ acceptor of the Calvin-Benson-Bassham cycle. Enolization of RuBP at the RuBisCO active site (coordinated by Mg²⁺ and a carbamylated Lys201 residue) generates a 2,3-enediolate intermediate that nucleophilically attacks CO₂.

Cofactors & Allosteric Regulation

Regenerated by Phosphoribulokinase (PRK). Competitive binding with CA1P in darkness; released by RuBisCO Activase (RCA) via ATP hydrolysis.

2. Net Export: 6(3C) - 1(3C G3P) = 15C Pool
3a. Aldolase I: 3C + 3C → 6C (FBP → F6P)
